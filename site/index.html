Install (once)

  1. Download the Add-in file above.
  2. Open Excel on the web (office.com) and open any workbook.
  3. Home → Add-ins → More Add-ins → My Add-ins → Upload My Add-in, then pick manifest.xml.
  4. Click Review on the Home tab.

Already installed? You don't need to download it again: the add-in updates itself. Close and reopen the Review panel to get the latest version.

Try the demo

  1. Open the Demo workbook in Excel and click Review.
  2. In the panel's Settings tab, set year-end to 31 Dec 2025 and threshold to 10000.
  3. Follow the workbook's Start here tab.

What's inside

✓ Workpaper basicsAlways on: errors, hard-codes, broken totals, sign-offs, purpose, source, tickmarks
🏦 Bank recRec vs GL, stale cheques, items cleared before year-end, large uncleared items
🔎 Unrecorded liabilitiesPost-year-end invoices for this year's services, pre-year-end invoices not in AP
📦 Revenue cut-offSales booked in the wrong year, credit notes after year-end
✉️ AR confirmationsUnexplained differences, non-responses with no follow-up, unsent confirms
🕘 Last year's notesFlags when last year's review notes are happening again
