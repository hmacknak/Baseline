Excel add-in for auditors

Live feedback on your workpaper, as you work.

Baseline Review reads the workpaper in front of you and tells you what is incomplete, what doesn’t add up and what the procedure is missing. Every flag comes with the reason behind it.

Three things, while the work is still open

Feedback that arrives while you can still act on it, instead of in a review note days later.

1

Workpaper basics

Fifteen checks on every sheet. Is it complete, is it consistent, does it add up? A total that skips a line, a blank in a testing table, a typed number among formulas, a date that doesn’t fit.

2

Procedure review, with the why

Baseline recognises the procedure and reviews the testing itself. Each flag explains why it matters, the assertion at risk and the standard behind it, so the feedback also teaches.

3

Last year’s review notes

Load last year’s notes once. Baseline shows which of those issues are happening again, on the flag itself, while there is still time to deal with them.

The reason, not just the flag

A flag on its own gets fixed and forgotten. Baseline says what is wrong, why a reviewer cares, which assertion is at risk and where the requirement comes from. The references follow the framework you choose: IFRS or US GAAP, with ISA and PCAOB auditing standards.

Invoices after year-end for this year’s services

D16Invoiced 9 Jan 2026 for services on 20 Dec 2025, not marked as recorded

Why it matters: the service was received before year-end, so the company already owed the money at year-end. If it isn’t recorded or accrued, liabilities and expenses are understated.

What it checks

The basics run on every sheet. Procedure reviews are offered when Baseline recognises the workpaper.

Workpaper basics

Formulas
Errors, hard-coded numbers, typed numbers among formulas, inconsistent formulas, links to other files
Totals
Totals that skip a line, numbers stored as text
Completeness
Blanks in testing tables, placeholders, purpose, source and conclusion
Dates
Prior-year dates left after roll-forward, year typos, dates typed as text; step through every date
Tidiness and sign-off
Leftover highlighting, stray numbers, tickmarks missing from the legend, dated sign-offs

Procedure reviews

Search for unrecorded liabilities
Post-year-end invoices for this year’s services, invoices missing from payables, over-accruals, missing service dates, a search window that stops too soon
Cash confirmations
Unexplained differences, no reply, replies not received directly from the bank, unconfirmed accounts, wrong confirmation date, overdrafts, foreign currency
Expense vouching
Amounts that don’t agree to support, cut-off, possible duplicate payments, missing approval, capital items expensed, wrong account

Get started

Install once. After that the add-in updates itself each time it opens.

Install

  1. Download the add-in file, manifest.xml.
  2. Open Excel on the web and open any workbook.
  3. Home → Add-ins → More Add-ins → My Add-ins → Upload My Add-in, then choose the file.
  4. Click Review on the Home tab.

Needs a current version of Excel: Microsoft 365 or Excel on the web.

Try it on the demo workbook

  1. Download the demo workbook and open it in Excel.
  2. Click Review, then set the year-end to 31 Dec 2025 in Settings.
  3. Follow the workbook’s Start here tab.
